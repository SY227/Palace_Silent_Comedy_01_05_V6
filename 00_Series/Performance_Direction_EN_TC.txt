V6 PERFORMANCE DIRECTION / 表演統一指引

Same stories, same heroine, same staging, same 15 seconds. Eight story-state images per episode. The upgrade is readable intention: every look has a target and every emotion has a cause.
保留故事、女主、場面調度及15秒長度，每集八個參考狀態。這版讓每次眼神有目標，每個表情有原因。

1. ATTEMPT / 嘗試
The supporting character is sincerely trying the wrong method. Narrowed but open eyes, a small brow pinch, compressed lips. Gaze at the relevant object. A viewer must see the mistake before seeing a reaction to it.
配角真心努力用錯方法。眼睛微瞇但睜開、眉心略皺、嘴唇輕抿，目光留在物件。觀眾要先看到錯法，再看到反應。

2. NOTICE / 發現
Her eyes move from the failed method to the available solution. One small raised eyebrow, neutral mouth. No smirk, eye-roll or exaggerated head shake. She is observant and kind.
她先看無效方法，再看現成解法，只輕抬一眉，嘴角中性。不譏笑、不翻白眼、不誇張搖頭，保留細心友善。

3. CONTACT / 動手
Her face becomes quietly focused. Keep hands, contact surface and relevant prop visible. The partner follows the action with their eyes. Do not turn faces toward the camera to show acting.
動手時表情專注平靜。手、接觸面及關鍵道具保持可見。配角眼睛追隨動作，不為了展示表情而轉向鏡頭。

4. CONFIRM / 確認
The physical result becomes visible first. Then the partner's brow releases and lifts slightly. Allow a short, readable pause on the changed prop; relief cannot precede the result.
先看見物理結果，配角眉心才放鬆並微抬。讓改變後的道具清楚停留一下；不要結果未發生就安心微笑。

5. RECOGNIZE / 恍然大悟
The partner looks from the working solution to their pointless method or their own hands. A small chin dip, relaxed shoulders and a brief pressed-lip smile say: I made this harder than it was. One dominant cue at a time.
配角由有效解法看回多餘方法或自己的手；輕低下巴、鬆肩、短暫抿嘴一笑，傳達「原來我搞複雜了」。每刻只保留一個主要訊號。

6. SIGNATURE / 招牌收尾
Only now does she allow a tiny warm closed-mouth smile. Empty flat palms meet at the lower sternum, fingers upward. One shallow bow, then settle. In cars, bow forward over her own lap with hips and knees forward. No extra flourish.
此時女主才淡淡閉嘴微笑。空的平掌在胸骨下方合攏，手指向上。小幅鞠躬一次後停穩；車內朝自己的膝上前彎，髖及膝維持朝前，不加花式動作。

Continuity over microdetail. If an eyebrow instruction competes with visible contact or the readable prop, preserve the contact and prop. Avoid constant smile, repeated blinks, long mutual gazes, romance, contempt, face deformation and new gestures.
連續性先於微小表情。眉眼指令若妨礙接觸或道具可見度，優先保留接觸及道具。避免全程微笑、連續眨眼、長時間互望、曖昧、輕蔑、臉變形或新增手勢。

The reference image is a sampled state; the video performs the transition naturally. Do not freeze for each image or cut eight times. Main story time windows remain unchanged.
參考圖是動作中的狀態，影片須自然過渡；不要每張停格或硬切八次。主故事時間窗維持原定安排。


SILENT VISUAL FOOTAGE ONLY. Neither character speaks. Keep both mouths softly CLOSED for the entire shot: no lip-sync, word-shaped mouth movements, moving jaw, visible teeth or tongue, open-mouth laughter, gasps, sighs or other human utterances. Act only through eyes, eyebrows, small head movements and posture. Generate NO AUDIO: no voice, narration, music or sound effects. The soundtrack is added separately in post.
只生成無聲畫面。兩名角色全程不說話，嘴唇自然輕閉：不對嘴、不做說話口型、不活動下顎、不露齒或舌頭、不張嘴笑、不喘氣、不嘆氣、不發任何人聲。只用眼神、眉毛、小幅頭部動作及姿態表演。不生成音訊：沒有人聲、旁白、音樂或音效；音軌另於後期加入。

POST-PRODUCTION ONLY: remove or disable the complete generated source-audio track before editing. Add one original or appropriately licensed relaxed instrumental jazz bed around 80 BPM: soft piano, sparse upright bass and brushed drums, continuous through the bow. No vocals, humming, scat, speech, laugh track or comedy sting. Add clean synchronized object Foley separately, with no human voices; keep music below useful contact sounds. Do not add jazz or Foley instructions to the silent generation prompt. If your interface explicitly offers audio-off, use it; no such UI or API setting is assumed here. Still inspect every face for talking-like animation.
僅後期處理：剪輯前先完整移除或停用生成素材的原音軌。另加一段原創或已取得適當授權、約每分鐘80拍的輕鬆爵士純演奏：柔和鋼琴、疏落低音提琴與刷奏爵士鼓，樂句連續至合掌。無歌聲、哼唱、擬聲唱、說話、罐頭笑聲或笑點提示音。另配乾淨、同步、無人聲的物件音效，爵士低於重要接觸聲。不要把爵士或音效製作指示貼入無聲畫面生成提示詞。若介面明確提供關閉音訊功能可使用，本包不假設存在該介面或API設定。仍須逐一檢查臉部有沒有說話動作。
