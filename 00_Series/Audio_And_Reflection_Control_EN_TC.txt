V6 AUDIO AND REFLECTION CONTROL / 音訊與反射控制

GENERATION / 生成
SILENT VISUAL FOOTAGE ONLY. Neither character speaks. Keep both mouths softly CLOSED for the entire shot: no lip-sync, word-shaped mouth movements, moving jaw, visible teeth or tongue, open-mouth laughter, gasps, sighs or other human utterances. Act only through eyes, eyebrows, small head movements and posture. Generate NO AUDIO: no voice, narration, music or sound effects. The soundtrack is added separately in post.
只生成無聲畫面。兩名角色全程不說話，嘴唇自然輕閉：不對嘴、不做說話口型、不活動下顎、不露齒或舌頭、不張嘴笑、不喘氣、不嘆氣、不發任何人聲。只用眼神、眉毛、小幅頭部動作及姿態表演。不生成音訊：沒有人聲、旁白、音樂或音效；音軌另於後期加入。

POST / 後期
POST-PRODUCTION ONLY: remove or disable the complete generated source-audio track before editing. Add one original or appropriately licensed relaxed instrumental jazz bed around 80 BPM: soft piano, sparse upright bass and brushed drums, continuous through the bow. No vocals, humming, scat, speech, laugh track or comedy sting. Add clean synchronized object Foley separately, with no human voices; keep music below useful contact sounds. Do not add jazz or Foley instructions to the silent generation prompt. If your interface explicitly offers audio-off, use it; no such UI or API setting is assumed here. Still inspect every face for talking-like animation.
僅後期處理：剪輯前先完整移除或停用生成素材的原音軌。另加一段原創或已取得適當授權、約每分鐘80拍的輕鬆爵士純演奏：柔和鋼琴、疏落低音提琴與刷奏爵士鼓，樂句連續至合掌。無歌聲、哼唱、擬聲唱、說話、罐頭笑聲或笑點提示音。另配乾淨、同步、無人聲的物件音效，爵士低於重要接觸聲。不要把爵士或音效製作指示貼入無聲畫面生成提示詞。若介面明確提供關閉音訊功能可使用，本包不假設存在該介面或API設定。仍須逐一檢查臉部有沒有說話動作。

ACCEPTANCE / 驗收
REJECT if either mouth opens or forms words, even when the clip is muted. Removing audio cannot repair visible talking. No written message, subtitles, captions, labels, logo, product mention or game interface.
任何角色張嘴或做說話口型，即使已靜音也須重做；刪除音訊不能修好說話動作。不要寫出故事訊息，不加字幕、字卡、標籤、標誌、產品名稱或遊戲介面。
Review the visible mouth in normal-speed playback, then frame-by-frame around any movement. Check the entire final audio track with headphones; zero spoken words or human utterances. Inspect the mirror at opening, just before contact, during rotation, immediately after release and during the final bow. A prompt is a direction, not proof that the generated take passed.
先正常播放檢查嘴部，再逐格看可疑動作。戴耳機聽完整最終音軌，不能有說話或人類發聲。鏡子須檢查開場、接觸前、轉動中、放手後及最終合掌；提示詞只是指令，不代表成片已通過。


Preserve existing glass transparency and reflection geometry. Do not invent reflected faces, duplicate people or impossible mirrored rooms. Keep incidental reflections simple and stable.
保留原有玻璃透明度與反射空間，不新增反射人臉、重複人物或不可能的鏡中房間；非故事重點的反射保持簡單穩定。
Generate no audio; no dialogue, narration or human utterances. Do not supply voice references or reference_audios. Jazz and clean object Foley are added separately in post after discarding source audio.
不生成音訊、對白、旁白或任何人聲。不提供聲音參考或reference_audios。先丟棄原音軌，再於後期另加爵士及乾淨物件音效。

Optional offline export helper: 04_Advanced/export_without_source_audio.py. It removes the original audio completely and can accept a separately supplied music track; this never fixes visible talking. Verify the actual final export is 15 seconds and 9:16. Do not stretch faces or blindly crop key action to force the ratio.
選用離線輸出工具：04_Advanced/export_without_source_audio.py。完整移除原音軌，可另用獨立提供的配樂；它不能修好可見說話動作。實際輸出須檢查15秒及9:16；不要拉伸臉部或盲目裁掉關鍵動作來硬湊比例。
