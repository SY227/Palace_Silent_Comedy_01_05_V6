EP01 / The Mirror Was Already There / 鏡子一直都在

A mirror already does this. / 鏡子本來就能做到。
Directing intent only; never written or spoken. / 只供導演參考，不顯示或說出。

A parked left-hand-drive car, engine off. A fixed rear-bench camera looks forward, slightly from the driver side. Man in vehicle-left driver seat; heroine in vehicle-right passenger seat. Hips, knees and feet face the dashboard; seatbacks remain behind shoulders. The phone sits below-left of the real BLACK-framed mirror, taped crookedly near the windshield; leave a visible gap between the devices. Phone screen and selfie camera face the empty rear cabin, showing only two beige rear headrests, the rear-window frame and a quiet rear courtyard with a plain brick wall, low green plants and stone paving behind the car, with no people or interface. The real mirror initially points too high and reflects only beige headliner. Front windshield retains the approved river/city sunset; that forward view is different from the rear courtyard. Phone, tape, mount, seats and camera never move.
左駕車停妥，引擎關閉。固定後排鏡頭略靠駕駛側向前拍；男子在車左駕駛座，女主在車右乘客座。骨盆、膝蓋及雙腳朝儀表板，椅背仍在肩後。手機歪貼於擋風玻璃附近，在真正黑框鏡子的左下方，兩者保留可見空隙。手機螢幕及自拍鏡頭朝空的後排，只顯示兩個米色後座頭枕、後窗框及車後安靜後庭院：素磚牆、低矮綠植與石鋪地面，沒有人或介面。真正鏡子起初朝得太高，只映米色車頂。前擋風玻璃保留原河景／城市夕陽，與車後庭院不同。手機、膠帶、鏡座、座椅及攝影機均不移動。

0-1.5s
Man already squints at the tiny taped phone, brow pinched, leaning forward. Make one small futile head tilt by 1.5s. Heroine observes the awkward method, not his face.
男子已前傾，睜眼瞇窄、皺眉看小手機，於第1.5秒前小幅歪頭一次仍無幫助；女主看錯誤方法，不看他的臉。

1.5-3s
He holds the awkward angle. Her eyes move from phone to real mirror, one raised eyebrow. LEFT fingertips hover beneath the BLACK mirror frame by 3s, without contact; reflection remains ceiling.
男子保持彆扭角度；女主由手機看真鏡，輕挑眉。第3秒左手指尖停黑色鏡框下方未接觸，鏡中仍是車頂。

3-5s
LEFT fingertips grip the BLACK mirror frame, not the phone. After visible contact, rotate once. By 5s the rear-courtyard reflection is settled and fingers still hold the frame. Eyes follow the real contact.
左手指尖握黑色鏡框、不碰手機；清楚接觸後才轉一次。第5秒後庭院反射已停穩，手仍握框；眼睛追隨真正接觸。

5-7s
LEFT fingertips release the frame by 7s, then descend clear of the phone. Mirror angle and rear view stay fixed. Man studies the working reflection; only now his brow relaxes.
第7秒左手指尖離框下降，避開手機。鏡子角度與後景不變；男子看有用反射，此時眉心才放鬆。

7-9s
LEFT hand returns to her lap. Man looks from the real mirror to the unnecessary phone and back; one slight eyebrow lift, no torso turn.
左手回自己膝上。男子由真鏡看多餘手機，再看回鏡子，只輕抬眉、不轉軀幹。

9-11.5s
He sits comfortably upright, checks the pointless tape, then dips his chin with a small sheepish closed-lip press. She calmly watches the working mirror.
男子坐直，看多餘膠帶後微低下巴、閉嘴輕抿，尷尬明白；她平靜看已能用的鏡子。

11.5-13.5s
He settles. She gives a tiny kind closed-mouth smile, facing forward, both hands separately on her lap. No exchange of looks.
男子停穩。她朝前淡淡友善閉嘴笑，雙手分開放膝上，不互相對望。

13.5-15s
She brings exactly two EMPTY flat palms visibly together at lower sternum, fingers upward. One shallow bow forward over her own lap, then hold. Keep the joined palms, fixed rear reflection and taped phone visible.
她恰好兩隻空手在胸骨下方清楚平貼合掌，指尖向上，朝自己膝上淺欠一次後停穩。合掌、固定後景反射與貼著手機均保持可見。
